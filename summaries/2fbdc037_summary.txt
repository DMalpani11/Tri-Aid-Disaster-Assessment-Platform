Kerala, India experienced severe flooding due to torrential monsoon rains starting in late May, with incessant downpours since August 8th. The event is described as the "worst floods" to hit the southern state.

As of the time of the report, the death toll had reached 370 since the end of May, and over 800,000 people were displaced to shelters. Including those displaced to relatives/friends homes, the total displaced is estimated to be over 1.5 million. Infrastructure losses are estimated at almost $3 billion. Many people are still living in partially flooded homes.

Rescue operations were underway, focusing on rescuing those marooned and airdropping supplies. The response involved thousands of army, air force, and navy troops, the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF), and approximately 500 local fishing boats. Fishermen were actively involved in rescuing people from interior lakes and rivers, sometimes at personal risk.

As of Sunday, when the rains subsided, 22,000 people were evacuated, but the overall disaster continues to affect millions of people, with ongoing relief efforts to help those stranded and displaced.
