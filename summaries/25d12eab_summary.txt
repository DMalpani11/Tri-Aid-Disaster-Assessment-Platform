Between August 8 and 16, Kerala experienced its worst floods in nearly a century. In the first 20 days of August, the state received 771 mm of rainfall, which was 179% more than the usual amount. The excessive rain caused 78 of the state's 80 dams, including those at Mullaperiyar and Idukki, to reach full capacity, forcing the opening of their sluice gates.

The sudden release of water led to severe flooding in several districts downstream, including Ernakulam, Idukki, Kottayam, Pathanamthitta, Alappuzha, and Thrissur. The floods affected the entire state, impacting highlands, cities, and lower plains.

The disaster resulted in over 400 deaths and displaced over 720,000 people, an amount roughly equivalent to the entire population of Puducherry. More than 40,000 hectares of farmland were submerged, and 26,000 houses were severely damaged. The floodwaters also inundated one of the state's three international airports, located in Kochi.
